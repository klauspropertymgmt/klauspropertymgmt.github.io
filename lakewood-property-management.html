About Lakewood

Lakewood is in East Dallas near White Rock Lake, known for tree-lined streets, the Lakewood Shopping Center and quick access to the lake's trails. It's one of the city's most established single-family neighborhoods.

Rentals in Lakewood

The homes: Mostly single-family homes, many built in the 1920s through the 1950s: Tudors, Craftsman bungalows and traditional brick homes, plus updated and new-build homes.

Demand: Single-family rentals here lease well, and renters tend to stay longer, which keeps turnover costs down.

What owners here should know

  • Older homes need attention: foundations, original plumbing, older electrical panels and trees near the roof. We inspect before listing and plan repairs with you.
  • Yard care and who handles it should be spelled out in the lease. We make that clear from day one.
  • Some streets fall in conservation districts with their own rules for exterior changes. We check before any exterior project.

What we handle for you

  • Pricing against current Lakewood rental comps
  • Photos, listing and syndication to the major rental sites
  • Showings, screening with written criteria, and a Texas-compliant lease
  • Rent collection and a clear monthly owner statement
  • Maintenance with trusted local vendors, at cost with no markup
  • HOA communication, renewals, move-out and deposit accounting

Thinking about furnished or short-term renting in Lakewood? Rules depend on your zoning, your HOA and the city. We check all three before we recommend a strategy.
