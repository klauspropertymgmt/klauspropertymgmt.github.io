FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Don't see yours? Call or text and we'll answer it.

What types of properties do you manage?

Condos, townhomes, single-family homes and small multifamily buildings across Dallas–Fort Worth, as long-term, furnished mid-term or short-term rentals.

How do you screen tenants?

Every applicant goes through credit, criminal background, eviction history, income verification and rental history checks, using the same written criteria for everyone.

How long does it take to lease my home?

A move-in-ready home typically leases in about four weeks, depending on price, condition and season.

Will I still have a say in decisions?

Yes. You approve larger repairs, lease renewals and any decision about eviction or selling. We handle the day-to-day.

Can you manage my short-term rental?

Yes. We list on Airbnb, VRBO and Booking.com, approve every booking by hand, and require a signed rental agreement and ID check from each guest.
