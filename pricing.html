Pricing FAQ

Questions about fees.

Do you charge a setup fee?

No. You pay the leasing fee when we place a resident, and management once rent is collected.

Do you mark up repairs?

No. You pay exactly what the vendor charges, and you get their invoice and photos.

What if my home is vacant?

No management fee is charged for months without rent.

I already have a tenant. Can you take over?

Yes. We review the existing lease, take over rent collection and maintenance, and there's no leasing fee because we didn't place the resident.

How do mid-term and short-term fees work?

They depend on furnishing, utilities, cleaning and how often the home turns over, so we quote each property. Ask for a free analysis and we'll include a quote.
