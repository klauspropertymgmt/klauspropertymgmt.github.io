Common questions

Why mid-term instead of a 12-month lease?

Furnished monthly rents are often higher, and stays of 30 nights or more generally aren't subject to the hotel occupancy taxes that nightly rentals pay. The trade-off is furnishing and turnover costs, and we'll run both scenarios with you.

Is mid-term allowed in my condo?

It depends on your HOA. Many set minimum lease terms. We read your HOA rules before we recommend it.

What does furnishing cost?

It depends on the size of the home and what you already have. We'll give you a furnishing checklist and budget as part of your rental analysis.
